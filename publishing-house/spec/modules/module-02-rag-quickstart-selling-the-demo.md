# Module 2: AI Quickstart RAG — Selling the Demo

### Brief Overview

This module puts participants in front of an already-deployed AI Quickstart RAG instance. Rather than deploying the QuickStart, participants follow a guide focused on *using* what's already running — exercising the RAG workflow and learning how to narrate it as a customer-facing demo.

### Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Red Hat Solution Architects, Red Hat One attendees
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Module 1 (what a QuickStart is, catalog navigation); access to the pre-deployed AI Quickstart RAG instance in the tenant namespace
- **Estimated duration:** 20 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Demonstrate the AI Quickstart RAG to a customer audience using a pre-deployed instance
- Identify the customer use case and value proposition the RAG QuickStart addresses
- Articulate the key talking points and moments to highlight when demoing this QuickStart to a customer

### Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Orientation to the pre-deployed AI Quickstart RAG environment | 5 min |
| 2 | Walk through the RAG workflow end-to-end | 10 min |
| 3 | Customer demo framing — talking points and what to highlight | 5 min |

### Detailed Steps

1. Confirm access to the pre-deployed AI Quickstart RAG instance in the participant's tenant namespace.
2. Tour the deployed environment (UI/console, key components) without redeploying anything.
3. Run through the RAG workflow as a customer would experience it, observing the retrieval and generation steps.
4. Identify the customer use case this QuickStart solves and the value proposition an SA would lead with.
5. Practice narrating the demo — what to show first, what questions customers typically ask, and how to tie it back to OpenShift AI.

### Key Takeaways

- The AI Quickstart RAG demonstrates a retrieval-augmented generation workflow customers can relate to their own knowledge base use cases
- Running the demo well is about narration and framing, not deployment steps
- Talking points connect the technical demo to the customer's business problem

### Infrastructure Notes

Requires the AI Quickstart RAG pre-deployed per tenant namespace via GitOps ahead of the session. No deployment steps performed by participants.
