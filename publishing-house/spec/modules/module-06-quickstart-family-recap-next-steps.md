# Module 6: QuickStart Family Recap & Next Steps

### Brief Overview

This closing module steps back from the two hands-on QuickStarts to tour the broader Red Hat QuickStarts family, giving participants a name/description/use-case/value-prop/link summary for each so they can match customer use cases to the right QuickStart. It ends with a concrete call to action for using QuickStarts in customer conversations going forward.

### Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Red Hat Solution Architects, Red Hat One attendees
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Completion of Modules 1–5
- **Estimated duration:** 10 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Analyze the broader QuickStart family to match customer use cases to the right QuickStart in a sales conversation
- Identify next steps and resources for using QuickStarts in customer engagements after the lab

### Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Tour the QuickStart family | 6 min |
| 2 | Match use cases to QuickStarts | 2 min |
| 3 | Next steps and call to action | 2 min |

### Detailed Steps

1. Present the broader Red Hat QuickStarts family beyond Secure Agent Workspace and Private AI Coding Assistant, covering for each: name, description, target use case, value proposition, and a link to its repository or catalog listing.
2. Compare the family members against the two QuickStarts covered hands-on in this lab, reinforcing where Secure Agent Workspace and Private AI Coding Assistant fit within the broader catalog.
3. Walk through a small set of representative customer scenarios and have participants identify which QuickStart in the family best matches each scenario.
4. Present the call to action: how participants can access, deploy, and demo QuickStarts on their own for upcoming customer engagements.
5. Point participants to resources (catalog links, upstream repos, internal enablement material) for continuing to build QuickStart-based demos after the lab.

### Key Takeaways

- The QuickStart family spans multiple customer use cases beyond the two demoed hands-on in this lab
- Matching a customer's stated problem to the right QuickStart is a core skill this lab is building toward
- SAs leave with a concrete next step for incorporating QuickStarts into upcoming customer conversations

### Infrastructure Notes

No hands-on deployment in this module; presentation/discussion only. No additional infrastructure beyond what was used in prior modules.
