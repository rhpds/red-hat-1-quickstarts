= Selling with Red Hat QuickStarts: A Hands-On Tour for Solution Architects
:imagesdir: ../images

Your customer doesn't want a slide about AI. They want to see it work on their problem. This lab shows you how to deliver that moment — fast — using Red Hat AI Quickstarts.

A QuickStart is a pre-built, deployable reference application that turns a Red Hat AI capability into something a customer can watch run. For a Solution Architect, it's a selling tool: no weeks of setup, no custom build, just a working system you can demo and then tie back to the platform underneath it. In the next 75 minutes you'll learn what a QuickStart is, run the flagship AI Quickstart RAG as a live customer demo, and shape the agentic story that moves a deal forward.

The whole lab is driven from the browser. The RAG application is already deployed and already loaded with data — you open the *RAG UI* tab and you're in. No console login, no commands, no setup in front of the customer.

== Who This Is For

This lab is written for Red Hat Solution Architects who already know their way around Red Hat OpenShift and Red Hat OpenShift AI, but haven't yet used QuickStarts as a way to sell. You should be comfortable navigating the OpenShift console and the basic ideas of projects and pods. You do not need any prior experience with RAG, vector databases, or agentic AI — the lab builds that as you go.

== What You'll Learn

* Explore the Red Hat AI Quickstarts catalog and identify how QuickStarts accelerate customer proofs-of-concept
* Run the AI Quickstart RAG as a customer-facing demo using a pre-deployed instance
* Demonstrate agentic RAG with live web search and narrate it as a customer sales story
* Position the RAG QuickStart's value back to Red Hat OpenShift AI in a sales conversation

== The Environment

You don't set anything up. The lab runs on Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform 4.21 with Red Hat OpenShift AI, and the AI Quickstart RAG is pre-deployed and pre-loaded with sample enterprise data served through Models-as-a-Service (MaaS). Everything you need is one tab away in the lab interface.

== Architecture

Under the demo, the RAG QuickStart ties retrieval, a vector store, and a served model together on Red Hat OpenShift AI. You don't need to configure any of it, but it helps to have the picture in mind when a customer asks what's running underneath.
